Privacy policy

Coach (test) · Last updated November 13, 2026

Test version. This app is being tested with a small group of people before it launches. This policy is a draft and has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer. It will be updated before the app is released to the public.

Who we are

Coach (test) is an AI fitness coaching app being built in Canada by Felipe Jimenez, who is responsible for your personal information (privacy officer). Contact: felipe_jimenez95@hotmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and password (the password is stored securely by our sign-in provider; we never see it).
  • Your consents: which consents you agreed to, when, and which version of the wording.
  • Your answers to the coach: your goals, training experience, schedule, equipment, and a short health screening (for example, known heart or other conditions, symptoms during exercise, injuries and pain, whether you are 65 or older, pregnancy). Height and weight only if you choose to share them.
  • What you log: sets, weights, reps and effort ratings, runs, Hyrox station times, extra activities, pain or soreness notes, session notes and exercise swaps.
  • Your weekly check-ins: how the week went, missed sessions, upcoming plans, pain or illness, sleep, stress, how your eating went and, if you choose, your bodyweight.
  • Nutrition details, only if you turn on nutrition targets: your age, sex, height, weight, everyday activity, your weight goal, whether a condition affects what you eat, and one question about eating disorders.
  • Your plans: the plans and changes the AI coach creates for you, and its reasons.
  • Technical records: how much AI processing each request used (for cost tracking), and basic crash and usage information collected by Apple TestFlight or Google if you install the test app.

Health information is sensitive. We only collect it after you give express consent in the app, and only what the coaching features need.

Why we use it

  • To build and adjust your training plan, and your nutrition targets if you turn them on.
  • To keep your training safe (for example, to stop programming and tell you to see a doctor if you report warning signs such as chest pain, dizziness or trouble breathing).
  • To run and improve the app during this test.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your plan is made by AI

Your plans are generated by an AI coach (Claude, made by Anthropic) that follows a written coaching rulebook. Every plan change shows a reason, and nothing changes until you approve it. You can ask a member of our team to review any plan change. The app is a wellness app: it does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and predictions are estimates.

Who else handles your information

  • Supabase stores the app's data on servers in Canada (Montréal region), with daily backups.
  • Anthropic processes your training information to create and adjust your plan. It may be processed outside Canada, including in the United States.
  • Expo builds the app and delivers app updates. It does not receive your training data.
  • Apple (TestFlight) and Google deliver the test app to your phone and may collect crash and usage information under their own privacy policies.
  • A member of our team may see your information only if you ask for a human review, or if a safety alert is created.

Information processed outside Canada may be accessible to authorities there under local law.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you use the app. You can ask us to delete it at any time (see below). Test data may also be deleted when the test ends; we will tell testers before that happens.

Your choices and rights

  • See, download, correct or delete your information.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (the app can't build plans without it).
  • Turn nutrition targets off at any time in the app.
  • Ask for a human review of any plan change.

In this test version there is no in-app button for downloading or deleting your data yet. Email felipe_jimenez95@hotmail.com and we will do it for you. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the privacy regulator in your province.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, each person can only access their own records, and sign-in details on your phone are stored in its secure storage. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Adults only

The app is for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

We will update this page when the app or the law changes, and ask for your consent again in the app when the change is meaningful.
